World — The Living Room · two options

A shoppable room. Hover anything we sell: the room dims, the object lifts out in white and a price tag appears. Figure, cards and wallpapers open a quick view, with a link through to the full product page. On touch, pulsing dots mark each item: tap for the tag, tap again to open. "Shop this room" lists everything for keyboard users, screen readers and search.

More prints, two ways (in both mockups). 1 — Rehang the wall: each frame holds a curated set of five prints. Hover a frame and use ‹ › to swap it; when nobody is touching the room, one frame quietly changes every few seconds. 2 — The print rack: a stack of frames leaning against the credenza holds the twelve prints that aren't on the wall, including every landscape print. Click it to flip through them with sizes and a Buy button; the frame in the room follows. That puts 27 prints in one room. The real print files are mapped into the photo's frames and lit by the room's own light; the room itself is AI-generated (Higgsfield), so the figure and card box are approximations.
