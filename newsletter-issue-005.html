The Land Is Green

Nigeria at 66

The Nigerian Edition

Today Nigeria is 66. This issue is for the country that sits underneath almost everything I make: its colour, its ceremony, its people and its stubborn, beautiful insistence on being itself.

A new piece that travelled further than I ever expected, a poster I art directed for a Nigerian story on a London stage, and something to say thank you.

01  ·  New print

The Land Is Green

The Land Is Green is a celebration of Nigeria, but it is also a reminder. The land is green. It has always been green. Regardless of who tried to hold it, control it, rename it or define it, the land never stopped belonging to itself. Its richness was never something that could be given back to us, because it was always ours. Thank you to everyone who has shared it this week.

02  ·  Art direction

Girls at the Young Vic

I had the pleasure of art directing the promotional cover for Girls, Theresa Ikoko's award-winning play, directed by Ewa Dina at the Young Vic. Nigeria, 2014: best friends Tisana, Haleema and Ruhab spend their days swapping notes on celebrities and dodging carbs, until they are kidnapped by militant insurgents. With only each other to hold on to, fear and religious difference begin to test a friendship that has to survive. The Maria Theatre, 22 October to 21 November.
