Pop-up motion
concepts

Three ways to bring the newsletter pop-up in. Each concept is shown twice — once on the homepage, where it rises from the bottom edge, and once on an inner page, where it comes down from under the nav. Same copy, same 25% offer, same Courier skin. Only the motion and the level of interruption change. Press replay on any frame, or slow everything to a quarter speed to inspect the easing.

HomepageUp from the bottom. First impression, full attention, dim behind it.
Inner pagesDown from the top. Second ask, lighter touch, never blocks the print.
TodayInstant display:flex 400ms after load, homepage only, every visit.
Frames rest in their settled state

Concept 01

Drift

Long expo-out travel, nothing else moving. It decelerates for almost the whole of its 900ms, so it reads as slow without ever feeling like it has stalled.

Motion
translateY only, 120px of travel, opacity 0→1
Duration
900ms card · 420ms dim
Easing
cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)
Homepage
Centred card rises from below the fold, dim overlay behind
Inner page
Full-width strip drops under the nav. No dim, no scroll lock
Dismiss
×, Esc, overlay click. Strip self-retracts after 12s
+Cheapest to build — two transitions, no new layout.
+The strip is genuinely ignorable, which is the point on repeat views.
−The safest of the three. Elegant, not memorable.

Concept 02

Slip

Paper sliding across a desk. Faster travel with a small overshoot, and a shadow that only appears as it settles — so the panel reads as a physical sheet landing on top of the page rather than a layer being faded in.

Motion
translateY with 1.22 overshoot + shadow on settle
Duration
700ms, shadow tracking the same curve
Easing
cubic-bezier(.22, 1.22, .36, 1)
Homepage
Full-width sheet docked to the bottom edge, never centred
Inner page
Card drops and docks flush under the nav
Dismiss
×, Esc, or scroll past 40% — slips back out the way it came
+Most personality; the overshoot gives it weight without being bouncy.
+A docked sheet leaves the hero print fully visible on the homepage.
−Shadows are a new material for this site — everything else is hairline rules.

Concept 03

Shutter

Nothing slides. The panel’s edge travels and the content behind it is revealed in place, like a blind being drawn — up from the bottom on the homepage, down from the top everywhere else. The slowest and the most editorial.

Motion
Height reveal; the 1px rule is the thing that moves
Duration
1100ms
Easing
cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1) — slow in, slow out
Homepage
Full-bleed plinth unrolls upward off the bottom edge
Inner page
Same panel unrolls downward from beneath the nav
Dismiss
Rolls back to zero height on close — no fade
+Unmistakably this site: it’s a moving rule, not a floating card.
+Full-bleed both ways, so home and inner are visibly the same object.
−Text sits still while the frame opens — needs a fixed panel height per breakpoint.
−At 1100ms it’s long enough to be felt on a slow connection.

Recommendation

Concept 01 for the mechanics, Concept 03 for the inner-page panel. Drift’s expo-out is the curve that actually delivers “slow” without dead time, and its inner-page strip is the only treatment here that a returning visitor can genuinely ignore. If you want the pop-up to feel like part of the site rather than a widget bolted onto it, take Shutter’s travelling rule for the inner pages and keep Drift on the homepage — they share a vocabulary. Concept 02 is the one to pick if you’d rather it had a bit of swagger.

Implementation

What has to change

Three blockers in the current build

  • display:none → flex can’t transition. index.html:270 toggles display, which is a binary property — no easing curve will ever apply. It has to become visibility + opacity + transform.
  • The pop-up only exists on the homepage. index.html is the only page with #modalOverlay. Inner pages need the markup before they can have a down-transition.
  • No frequency cap. setTimeout(openModal, 400) at index.html:1428 fires on every single load, including for people who already subscribed.

Suggested rules

  • Homepage: 1.2s after load — long enough for the hero to land first.
  • Inner pages: on the second page view of a session, not the first.
  • Cap at once per 14 days; suppress permanently once fa_subscribed is set.
  • Never on /claim, /verify/*, /guess — those are campaign landings with their own call to action.
  • Stack below the cookie banner, which already owns the bottom edge at z-index:10000.
/* replaces .modal-overlay { display:none } */
.modal-overlay{
  position:fixed; inset:0; z-index:300;
  display:flex; align-items:center; justify-content:center; padding:20px;
  background:rgba(0,0,0,0);
  visibility:hidden;                      /* keeps it out of the tab order */
  transition:background .42s linear, visibility 0s linear .9s;
}
.modal-overlay.open{
  background:rgba(0,0,0,.45);
  visibility:visible;
  transition:background .42s linear, visibility 0s;
}
.modal{                                   /* homepage: up from the bottom */
  transform:translateY(120px); opacity:0;
  transition:transform .9s cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), opacity .9s linear;
}
.modal-overlay.open .modal{ transform:translateY(0); opacity:1 }

.modal-overlay.from-top .modal{           /* inner pages: down from the top */
  transform:translateY(-120px);
}
@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce){
  .modal{ transform:none !important; transition:opacity .2s linear }
}
