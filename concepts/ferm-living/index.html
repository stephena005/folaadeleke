Ethnic Isle × Ferm Living

Homeware concept — not currently in production

Enquire About This Collaboration

What would the future look like if Africa were allowed to design itself? Ethnic Isle is an imagined collaboration between Fola and Ferm Living — cultures from across Africa met without borders, where tradition isn’t preserved behind glass but continues to evolve through everyday living. Home, here, is the feeling of being understood without explanation.

The Idea

“For generations, Africa has inspired the world while rarely receiving the same recognition for its own design philosophies. Yet our homes have always carried stories. Ethnic Isle is my attempt to build that feeling.” — Fola

Kuba textiles → the Kuba Shelf

Inspired by the rhythmic geometry of Kuba textiles and the continuous weaving traditions found across Central and West Africa, the shelving system transforms decorative pattern into load-bearing structure — storage, structure and storytelling as one piece.
