Fola Adeleke × Ferm Living

“Ethnic Isle” — an imagined homeware collection

What would the future look like if Africa were allowed to design itself?

For generations, Africa has inspired the world while rarely receiving the same recognition for its own design philosophies. Yet our homes have always carried stories. Ethnic Isle — the title speaks for itself — is cultures from across Africa meeting without borders, where tradition is not preserved behind glass and continues to evolve through everyday living. It is an imagined destination where African creativity occupies the space it has always deserved.

For me, home is comfortability and identity. Home is the feeling of being understood without explanation. Ethnic Isle is my attempt to build that feeling.

The Pieces

Kuba Shelf

Inspired by the rhythmic geometry of Kuba textiles and the continuous weaving traditions found across Central and West Africa. The shelving system transforms decorative pattern into load-bearing structure — storage, structure and storytelling becoming one.

Isale Sofa

A low, angled frame in dark wood carrying cushions patterned with the same honeycomb motif that runs through the collection — natural, simple, and built to work in any room.

Ayo Lounge Chair

A wider companion piece to the Isale Sofa, sharing its angled joinery and upholstery language while offering room for two.

Rafia Chair

Fully hand-wrapped in natural fibre, drawing on woven basket and rafia traditions — simple, strong, natural and timeless from every angle.

Olumole Ceiling Lamp

A hand-knotted jute pendant built from the same hexagonal weave found across the collection, softening the light it holds.

ORIKI Rug

Woven portraiture bordered in hand-knotted fringe — a floor piece that carries a story rather than simply grounding a room.

This is a speculative design collaboration imagined by Fola Adeleke and is not an official Ferm Living product line. None of the pieces shown are currently for sale. For collaboration enquiries, get in touch.
