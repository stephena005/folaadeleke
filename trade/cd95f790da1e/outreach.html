Outreach sequence — hotels, restaurants, clubs

Plain text, sent from a personal address, no HTML template. One image inline (the render nearest their venue type), the lookbook PDF attached or linked. Replace everything in [brackets] before sending; the bracketed detail is the whole point.

Sequence: Email 1 → wait 5 days → Follow-up 1 → wait 9 days → Follow-up 2 → stop.

Follow-ups for studios use the same day 5 and day 14 emails above, with "a project in fit-out" in place of "the wall".

Notes on sending

- One venue per email. Never BCC a list; the bracketed detail is what earns the reply.
- The first line about the wall must be true. Go and look at it, or use their Instagram and say so.
- Attach the PDF to email 1 only. It is 14 MB; if the domain bounces large attachments, link it instead.
- Send Tuesday to Thursday, mid-morning. Restaurants: never during service.
- Log every send and reply in the target list so the follow-up timing is honest.
