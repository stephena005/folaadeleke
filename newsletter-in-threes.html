Monochrome · Camouflage · Indigo

In Threes

On the gallery wall the prints hang by subject: love, family, the function. This issue cuts across that. Here are nine works grouped by how they look — the cloth, the colour, the way the figure sits in the frame.

Each set is made to hang together. Buy one and it stands on its own; buy three and the wall starts a conversation.

01  ·  Monochrome

Black, white and loud

No colour to hide behind. Zebra cloth in black and white, worn and hung and laid out until the stripes become the room. These three are the most graphic things I make: they hold from across a room and they hold in a hallway.

Centre of the Stage

Sisterhood

No VC / No Vacation

Hang them as a row at the same size. The stripes run into each other and the wall starts to move.

02  ·  Camouflage

Drowned in cloth

Figure and ground cut from the same fabric. The sitter is wrapped in the pattern the wall is papered in, so you find the face last. Gold honeycomb for the twins, deep red for the third.

Drowned — Kehinde

Drowned — Taiwo

Drowned in Native

Kehinde and Taiwo were made as a pair and want to hang side by side. Drowned in Native finishes the set.

03  ·  Indigo

Blue hour

Everything set in blue: the backdrop, the agbada, the gown. The light is cool and the gold reads warmer for it. The quietest of the three groupings, and the one that sits best in a bedroom.

I Do

Bound

Throne

I Do is tallest, so hang it in the middle with Bound and Throne either side.
