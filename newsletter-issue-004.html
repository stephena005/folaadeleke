02  ·  Concept Collaboration

Ethnic Isle × Ferm Living

What would the future look like if Africa were allowed to design itself? An imagined collaboration with Ferm Living — the Kuba Shelf, Isale Sofa, Ayo Lounge Chair, Rafia Chair and the ORIKI Rug, built from pottery, woven baskets, natural fibres and handmade joinery. Concept only, for now.

03  ·  Concept Kit

Reimagining Through African Couture

Football is one of the world’s greatest cultural artefacts. I wanted to imagine what it would become if it were inherited through African couture craftsmanship rather than performance design. This piece isn’t designed to perform; it exists as a statement, inviting the viewer to experience the sport through the language of fashion and craftsmanship.

“These garments don’t exist to perform. They exist to be admired.”

04  ·  New Print

The Crown She Carries

This piece is about the beauty that exists within the chaos of African life — the strength, softness, responsibility and resilience that can all exist at once. It speaks to carrying more than what is visible, and still finding a way to move through it with grace. Edition of 15, in A1, A2 and A3.

05  ·  New Print

State of the Union

What happens when two different cultural ideas of tradition and union exist in the same space. Instead of choosing one over the other, I wanted to bring both together and let them create something new. It’s about identity, influence and how culture can shift without losing where it comes from. Edition of 15, in A1, A2 and A3.
